# Pocketorio: Factorio on iPhone Duo and iPad

An experimental port that runs **your own copy of Factorio for macOS** natively on an
iPhone Duo or an iPad. One app downloads the game you bought on Steam, adapts it and
signs it, all on the device. Factorio's own engine and Metal renderer run unchanged,
with touch controls, an on-screen keyboard, a portrait layout, and Bluetooth keyboard
and trackpad support. Saves can sync with your PC through Steam Cloud.

## What you need

- An **iPhone Duo (iOS 27.2 recommended)** or an **iPad with an M1 chip or newer
  (iPadOS 26+)**. Other devices are untested.
- **Factorio on Steam**, and your Steam account.
- An **Apple Account** (a free one works; the app then has to be renewed every 7 days,
  which SideStore does for you).
- For the Xcode route: an Apple Silicon Mac with about 40 GB free and Xcode 27.2 or
  later. For the SideStore route, no Mac after setting up SideStore.

| Device | Status |
|---|---|
| iPhone Duo | Plays: Space Age menu, saves, touch, Bluetooth keyboard and trackpad |
| iPad (M1 or newer) | Plays: tutorial, mining, inventory, text entry, saving and loading, audio |

## Install

There are two ways to get the app onto the device.

- **SideStore** (no Mac needed after setup): add the source
  `https://github.com/Salzanoo/Pocketorio/releases/latest/download/apps.json` to
  SideStore and install from it. See [SideStore](docs/SIDESTORE.md).
- **Xcode** (on a Mac): double-click **`Install Factorio.command`**. The step-by-step
  [install guide](docs/INSTALL.md) starts from installing Xcode.

Either way, the app then asks you to sign in to Steam, import the signing certificate,
and download and prepare Factorio on the device.

## How it works, in short

The app downloads Factorio's macOS build from Steam with your account, checks it
against a list of audited builds (newer minor versions are offered as untested), and
patches a copy of the executable for iOS: its SDL calls go to an iOS build of SDL, and
the few macOS functions it needs are filled in. The patched executable becomes a
library, is signed on the device with your certificate, and the app loads it and runs
Factorio's `main`. Nothing of Factorio is in this repository or in the app you install.
See [Architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) and [On-device patching](docs/ON_DEVICE.md).

## Status and limitations

Large factories, long sessions, multiplayer, controllers, fold and unfold while
running, and the full range of building actions are untested. Steam features in the
game (achievements, workshop) aren't available. A Factorio update can break the port
until it is audited; the app offers newer minor versions as untested and says why one
doesn't load. See [Architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#verified-behavior-and-remaining-gaps).
